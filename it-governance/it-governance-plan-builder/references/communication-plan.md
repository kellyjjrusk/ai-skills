# Communication plan

Policies without communication have no value. The Internal Communications Lead owns this.

## Principles

- **Know the audience.** Leaders, managers and practitioners need different things.
- **Answer "what's in it for me?"** in the first two lines.
- **Be brief.** Bullets for leaders and managers; steps and FAQs for practitioners.
- **Use more than one angle and channel** (email, chat, wiki, team meetings, training).
- **Time it.** Enough lead time to act; not so many messages that people tune out. Bundle minor changes.
- **Close the loop.** One place to ask questions; review questions at every committee meeting.

## Audience matrix

| Audience | Content | Method | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Senior leadership | Organizational impact, risk reduced, decisions needed | Concise bulleted email | Before the leadership meeting where it's discussed |
| Middle managers | How to reinforce the message and confirm compliance | Concise bulleted email with FAQs | Two emails, one week apart |
| Practitioners (implementers, users, admins) | Implementation steps, impact on daily work, escalation points | Longer email with steps, FAQs/links; chat updates | Three emails plus chat updates, one week apart |
| Vendors / partners | Contractual or operational obligations | Formal notice via vendor manager | ≥ 30 days ahead |
| New joiners | Policies for their role | Onboarding pack and training | Before access |

## Templates

### Leadership email

> **Subject:** {App} governance: what changes on {date}
>
> - **Why:** {one-line risk or business reason}
> - **What:** {policy names, one line each}
> - **Impact:** {teams affected, effort}
> - **Ask:** {decision or support needed}

### Manager brief

> **Subject:** Action for your team: {App} {policy} from {date}
>
> **What's changing:** … **What your team must do:** … **How to check:** {report/metric} **Questions:** {channel}
>
> FAQ: {3–5 likely questions with answers}

### Practitioner guide

> **Subject:** How {App} {process} works from {date}
>
> **Why it helps you:** … **Steps:** 1… 2… 3… **Where to get help:** … **If something breaks:** {escalation contact}
>
> FAQ and links: …

### Change notice (standard update)

> **{Policy ID} standard updated** — {what changed}, effective {date}. No action needed / Action: {step}. Details: {link}.
